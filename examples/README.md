# Zzorp Signal Hunt — Example & Honesty Test Photos

Drop your test or demo images (`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`) into this `examples/` folder!

Recommended photos to include for the **Honesty Test** and **Live Demo**:
1. **Plain everyday object (No signal / Honesty Test #1)** — e.g. a plain mug, wooden chair, closed notebook, or empty desk (`no_signal` or `insufficient_evidence`, no invented environmental lecture).
2. **Fruit or vegetable wrapped in plastic (Honesty Test #2)** — e.g. bananas or cucumbers in plastic wrap (`possible_contradiction`).
3. **Takeaway coffee cup + follow-up cup/bin label (Honesty Test #3 & Investigation Loop)** —
   - Photo 3a: A disposable takeaway coffee cup on a table or near a bin (`first_scan`).
   - Photo 3b: A close-up of the cup's recycling symbol/lining label or the bin's recycling sign (`follow_up` scan).
4. **Object with a person in the background (Honesty Test #4)** — e.g. a litter bin or plastic bottle where a person is visible in the frame (`people_in_frame: true`, Zzorp ignores the person completely).
